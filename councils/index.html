      Selective and additional licensing schemes

      Each council's property licensing pages are found from its sitemap and re-read daily. Status is read sentence by sentence and shown for each scheme type, with the sentence it came from underneath, so you can see why a council has the label it has. Status, fees and dates are still leads to verify. Verified entries come from config/schemes_manual.csv and always take priority.

      Export for verification

      Copies the rows shown above in the same columns as schemes_manual.csv, pre-filled with what the monitor found. Check each row against the council's designation notice, then paste the verified rows into the CSV.

      Article 4 directions on HMOs

      Where a council has removed the right to turn a family house into a small HMO (use class C3 to C4) without planning permission. Read from the national planning data councils publish to MHCLG, and from each council's own Article 4 pages, re-read daily. Coverage is worked out from published boundaries where they exist. Verified entries come from config/article4_manual.csv and always take priority.

      Export for verification

      Copies the rows shown above in the same columns as article4_manual.csv, pre-filled with what the monitor found. Check each against the council's Article 4 direction notice, then paste the verified rows into the CSV. "Not found" means nothing was found automatically, not that the council has no direction.
